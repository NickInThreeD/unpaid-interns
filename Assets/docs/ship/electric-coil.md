# Electric Coil

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Electric_Coil

## Overview

The Electric Coil (also referred to as the battery charger) is a device mounted to the wall of the ship in *Lethal Company* that **instantly recharges battery-powered items**. It is standard equipment, requires no purchase, and is the reason battery-limited tools remain viable across a full day — provided someone returns to the ship to top them up.

## Key Points

- Mounted on the ship wall.
- **Recharges instantly** — there is no charging time.
- **Only items that actually have a battery can be charged.**

## Rechargeable Items

- Flashlight
- Pro-Flashlight
- Laser Pointer
- Walkie-Talkie
- Hairdryer
- Jetpack
- Zap Gun
- Boombox

Note that this list spans store equipment (Flashlight, Pro-Flashlight, Walkie-Talkie, Jetpack, Zap Gun, Boombox) and scrap items that happen to have batteries (Laser Pointer, Hairdryer).

## Known Bugs

Two related timing bugs are documented, both likely unintentional:

- If you hold a chargeable item, switch to a different inventory slot, and **quickly press the charge button**, your character still performs the charging animation (without the sound effect). This applies to all items and scrap **except two-handed scrap**.
- The inverse also occurs: holding a non-chargeable item and then switching to a chargeable one leaves you **briefly unable to charge** it.
