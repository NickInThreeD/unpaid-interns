# Intercom

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Intercom

## Overview

The Intercom is a device present both in the autopilot ship and at Gordion (The Company) that plays automatic recorded announcements from The Company. It is a purely narrative/informational system with a small number of scripted triggers, though it interacts with a couple of other mechanics worth knowing.

## Activation Triggers

The intercom only activates in four situations:

1. **A new save file is created** — the onboarding broadcast.
2. **Scrap is sold** to The Company.
3. **It is the last day of the current profit quota** — the deadline reminder.
4. **Employees are dismissed** — the game-over message.

In multiplayer, **every new player joining a completely new file hears the onboarding broadcast once**.

The intercom on the ship **can be turned off mid-message**.

## Broadcast Content

### Game start

The onboarding message welcomes the employee, explains that the autopilot ship is where they will live and sleep for the duration of the contract, and directs them to check the instruction manual and sign into the terminal. Notably, a rapid-fire section — played at roughly **5× speed** — states that the ship belongs to the Company, that the employee only holds temporary usage rights, that all actions aboard are supervised, and that the Company can remotely operate the ship when necessary. The message then degrades into a stuttering, rap-like loop of "great asset to the company."

### Quota reminder

Instructs the crew to return to the Company Building **immediately** to sell their scrap, stating they have **zero days left** to meet the profit quota.

### Game over

"As you have not met the profit quota, your performance has been deemed below standard. Welcome to our disciplinary process."

### Scrap sold

Five default voice lines can play after a sale, with **rare variants having roughly a 3% chance** of replacing a regular one. These come from the intercom **at the Company**, not the one in the ship.

## Notes

- The intercom **can be activated and deactivated while holding a two-handed item**, unlike most ship controls.
- **A lightning strike on the ship shuts the intercom off.**
- In theory a broadcast could alert Eyeless Dogs, but in practice it cannot — any in-progress message or voice line is automatically cut off when the lever is pulled.
