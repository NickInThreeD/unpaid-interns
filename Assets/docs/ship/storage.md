# Storage

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Storage

## Overview

Storage is the *Lethal Company* feature that holds ship items and cosmetics that have been packed away. Pressing **[X]** while moving a ship object stores it; the stored inventory is viewed by typing **`STORAGE`** into the terminal. This lets a crew buy ship upgrades and decor without permanently cluttering the ship's limited floor space.

## Controls

While in the object-moving mode:

- **[B]** — interact with an object (enter move mode).
- **[X]** — store the object.
- **[R]** — rotate the object.
- **[B]** — confirm placement.

## What Can Be Stored

Both purchased and default ship contents can be stored.

### Ship upgrades

- **Teleporter (375)** — teleports whoever is on the ship's radar back to the ship; they lose all held items in transit. Roughly ten seconds to recharge.
- **Inverse Teleporter (425)** — teleports you to a random position outside the ship, dropping all items at the pad first. Usable by everyone at once. The store listing quotes a **3.5-minute cooldown** (the ship page lists 5 minutes). Its in-game disclaimer warns it **only transports you in, not out, and you may become trapped**.
- **Signal Translator (255)** — broadcast a text message under 10 letters to all crewmates via the `TRANSMIT` command.
- **Loud Horn (100)** — communicate with any crew member at any distance without a walkie-talkie; audible from anywhere.

### Default furnishings

- **Cupboard** — stores small items.
- **File Cabinet** — decorative small cabinet.
- **Bunkbeds** — decorative.

### Purchasable furnishings

Shower (180), Table (90, a good surface to stack items on), Jack O' Lantern (50), Television (130, plays company safety tips), Record Player (120), Romantic Table (120), Toilet (150, flushes when interacted with), Welcome Mat (40), Plushie Pajama Man (100), Disco Ball (150), and Goldfish (50).

### Noise warning

Two furnishings interact with entity hearing in opposite ways:

- **Plushie Pajama Man** squeaks when interacted with but **does not alert Eyeless Dogs.**
- **Disco Ball** plays music and emits colored lights when the ship lights are off, and **does alert Eyeless Dogs** — a serious hazard, since a continuous noise source can hold a dog inside the ship indefinitely.

## What Cannot Be Stored

Two objects still display as storable when you press [B] on them, but cannot actually be stored:

- **Light switch** (default) — turns lights on and off.
- **Terminal** (default) — used for everything.
