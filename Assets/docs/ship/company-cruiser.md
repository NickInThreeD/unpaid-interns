# Company Cruiser

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Company_Cruiser

## Overview

The Company Cruiser is the first and currently only vehicle in *Lethal Company* — a red truck with a two-seat cab and a large loading bay. It is bought from the Store for **370 credits (host) / 400 (client display)** and delivered by the Dropship on ropes. It transforms scrap logistics by making two-handed items transportable in bulk, provides near-total protection from most outdoor entities, and doubles as a weapon. It is also fragile, explosive, and demanding to drive.

## Key Points

- **Type:** Specialized Gear. **Battery life:** unlimited. **Conductive:** No — **lightning cannot strike through the truck.**
- **Damage:** instant kill to entities; varies for employees.
- Ordering the Cruiser **occupies the entire Dropship shipment** — items must be ordered in a separate delivery.
- A **Service Manual** clipboard in the loading bay explains its functions and the ways it can explode.
- The Cruiser **blocks line of sight to enemies** and comes with a free lifetime warranty allowing free replacement.

## Controls

- **[W]** — gas pedal
- **[S]** — brake pedal
- **[Space]** — boost

## Core Systems

### Driving mode

Sit in the driver's seat and **hold [E]** to insert the key. Starting usually takes a few tries. Once running, the driver's hands are locked to the wheel; removing the key exits driving mode. Both key insertion and removal can be interrupted by releasing [E]. Steering uses [A] and [D].

### Gears

- **Drive** — normal operation.
- **Reverse** — steering is inverted and [W] moves the truck backwards.
- **Park** — the Cruiser slides around less, but **using the throttle in Park will cause the Cruiser to explode.**

### Hood and health

Opening the hood reveals two gauges: an **orange turbo boost** gauge and a **green health** gauge. Health drops from collisions and from throttling while in Park. **When the green gauge empties, the Cruiser explodes.**

- **Entering orbit fully resets Cruiser health.**
- **Weed Killer applied to the engine repairs it.** If health is already full, the Weed Killer instead adds turbo boost.

### Boost

Jumping in the driver's seat lifts the Cruiser slightly; with turbo boost remaining, it lifts significantly higher. Useful for dislodging the truck from rocks and trees, or jumping small gaps.

### Loading bay

A large cuboid bed behind the cab that can hold **a full crew plus unlimited items and scrap**. Two openings feed it: a small cabin window at the rear of the cab (too small for employees) and the rear door, which slides up like a garage door.

### Cab and doors

Two seats. The cab has no roof, so employees can sit without opening the doors. **Whether a door is open determines whether entities can attack the employee on that side** — an employee in the passenger seat is protected as long as the *passenger* door is closed, regardless of the driver's door.

### Other cab functions

Headlight toggle (weak cab lighting), cabin window toggle (synchronized with the other bay inlet), **wipers (currently serve no purpose, since there is no roof)**, radio, honk, and eject.

### Radio

Four public-domain tracks — "Yes, We Have No Bananas", "The Parade of the Wooden Soldiers", "Some Sunny Day", and "West Indies Blues". One button tunes, another toggles. **The radio alerts Eyeless Dogs.**

### Honk

Held on the steering wheel center, producing a loud blare that **attracts Eyeless Dogs** — tactically useful for pulling dogs off teammates.

### Eject

A red button under a glass cap on the cab panel. Holding it launches the **driver** high into the air, dealing massive fall damage on landing — unless there is a higher platform to catch them, in which case damage is minimal. **Both the driver and the passenger can press it.** Used for emergency escapes, fast travel, and reaching high ground. With the Cruiser on its side, eject sends you horizontally for very fast travel.

### Push

Employees can push the Cruiser from any angle to free it from tight spots. **Push force is reduced while in Park.**

## Entity Interactions

**Seated employees are invincible to most entities.** From a code perspective the Cruiser kills every entity it hits; some simply lack a death animation, so the kill can't be visually confirmed (Earth Leviathans, Circuit Bees, and Roaming Locusts). The one true exception is the **Ghost Girl**, who has no collision box and is entirely unaffected.

**Cannot harm seated employees:** Eyeless Dog, Baboon Hawk, Kidnapper Fox, Masked (in most situations).

**Can harm seated employees:**
- **Forest Keeper** — the only entity that can see employees seated in the cab.
- **Old Bird** — grab and missile attacks damage seated employees; stomp and squish attacks flip the Cruiser.
- **Circuit Bees** — hurt seated employees despite being crushable while chasing.

**Can flip or overturn the Cruiser:** Eyeless Dogs (charging into it), Baboon Hawks, Old Birds.

**Special cases:**
- **Earth Leviathan** — can be killed by the Cruiser, but its emerging animation continues, and it will still devour a stationary Cruiser along with the employees, scrap, and items inside.
- **Masked** — cannot be run over; they are pushed away instead. Attempting to drive over them (especially in reverse) can let one reach the driver and convert them into another Masked.

Indoor-only entities (Hoarding Bug, Snare Flea, Bracken, Bunker Spider, Thumper, Nutcracker, Butler, Spore Lizard, Coil-Head, Jester, Hygrodere, Barber, Lasso Man) cannot normally interact with the Cruiser at all.

## Strategy

### Transportation

The Cruiser is the safest and most efficient transport for large hauls, surpassing the Jetpack. Before Version 55, two-handed scrap had to be moved on foot because the Jetpack couldn't carry it; the Cruiser's trunk solved this.

**Efficiency depends entirely on driver skill.** Poor driving risks falling into Vow's canyon or March's lake, flipping into pits, or driving into quicksand. **Drive slowly.** Inexperienced drivers should not use it as a primary mobility tool — bumpy, risky movement risks explosion.

**Moon suitability:**
- **Best:** Gordion and Embrion (vast flat terrain, ideal for practice); Offense is slightly harder but still convenient.
- **Pointless:** Titan, whose effective route is vertical — the Cruiser cannot drive staircases.
- **Not recommended:** Rend and Dine, due to very low visibility and hilly terrain.
- **Near useless:** Adamance, because of the fragile bridge connecting the map's two halves.

### The Cruiser jump

A popular technique for returning the Cruiser and its cargo to the ship, especially on **Artifice** and **Rend** where ship-to-fire-exit distances are long. It requires precise timing, practice, and a memorized lineup. **Fill the boost gauge with Weed Killer first.** Critically: **the Cruiser explodes on impact with the ship if the ship's magnet is not engaged.**

### Combat use

The Cruiser can clear outdoor entities, but this is high-risk — **do not carry valuable scrap during attack runs.** Better practice is one dedicated employee attacking outdoor threats while the others haul scrap.

Colliding with an entity temporarily slows or stops the Cruiser, giving other entities an opening. Against entities that *can* reach seated employees, the eject button is the last-resort escape.

**Armored turret setup:** stock the trunk with Shovels, Stun Grenades, Double-Barrels, Kitchen Knives, and Shotgun Shells so passengers can fight while the driver charges.

**The best documented tactic:** place a **Radar-Booster** in the trunk. Whoever is on camera duty can then view the Cruiser's surroundings at any time and use `FLASH` to stun threatening entities.

## Notes and Warnings

- **Being crushed by the Cruiser during delivery deals 80 damage.**
- Once purchased, the Cruiser attaches to the side of the ship via a **large magnet**, toggled by a lever behind the ship, allowing transport between moons. The magnet can also pull employees toward it.
- The Cruiser can break trees if driven fast enough, but **power lines (as on Artifice) cannot be broken.**
- **The Company Monster cannot hear the Cruiser's engine noises.**
- **Known bug:** items dropped on the Cruiser floor by clients phase through it.
- Using a Jetpack while in the trunk makes the Cruiser move erratically.
- **On Assurance**, the Dropship delivers the Cruiser onto a high rock, from which it slides off due to its large collision box — it can still land upright and undamaged.

## Trivia

The radio's track selection is likely chosen to avoid copyright issues, all pieces being decades past their protection period. A Zeekerss teaser showed the Cruiser running over a *sleeping* Eyeless Dog — sleeping dogs do not exist in game.
