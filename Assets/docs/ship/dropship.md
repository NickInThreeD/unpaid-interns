# Dropship

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Dropship

## Overview

The Dropship, or Transport Vehicle, is the rocket that delivers items ordered through the ship's terminal in *Lethal Company*. Its arrival is loud, time-limited, and dangerous — the 30-second retrieval window, its lethal landing, and its ability to attract Eyeless Dogs all make it a tactical event rather than a simple delivery.

## Key Points

- Items bought while landed on a moon are delivered **to that moon**.
- Items bought **while in orbit** are delivered to the **next moon the ship lands on**.
- On landing, a locating melody plays for **30 seconds**.
- **A maximum of 12 items can be delivered per dropship.**

## Delivery and Retrieval

The dropship lands at a designated spot on the moon. Employees interact with one of **four storage compartments** on the exterior sides of the vehicle to open the doors and take out their items.

**After 30 seconds the rocket abruptly leaves.** Anything not retrieved is kept by the rocket and permanently lost — retrieve quickly.

### Ordering constraints

- Additional items cannot be ordered until the current dropship's items are delivered and/or it has left.
- **Ordering the Company Cruiser occupies the dropship's entire capacity**, blocking further orders until the cruiser is delivered.
- If items are ordered while in orbit or immediately on landing, the dropship begins its descent around **8:30 AM** and lands around **8:45 AM**. Assuming retrieval by roughly 9:00 AM, ordering costs about **one in-game hour of delay**.

## Warnings

- **Employees standing under the rocket when it lands are crushed and killed.** Clear the landing pad.
- **The rocket's landing attracts Eyeless Dogs** to the sound.

## Tactical Use

The Eyeless Dog attraction is exploitable. A player on the ship can purchase **the cheapest available item** purely to trigger a dropship landing, drawing Eyeless Dogs away from where the crew is working or travelling. Account for the delay between placing the order and the actual landing when timing this.

This is the same technique recommended for eclipsed moons — buying a cheap item before landing so the delivery distracts dogs while the crew enters the facility.

## Audio

The dropship's melody is the first verse of **"Turkey in the Straw"**, the folk song commonly used by ice cream trucks; it is easily confused with "Do Your Ears Hang Low?", which differs only in the final bar. Distinct variants exist for playing far away or inside the facility, an older v4 version, a **holiday version** (fairy lights on the rocket and an arrangement of "We Wish You a Merry Christmas"), and a **faster, distorted version played when delivering the Company Cruiser**.

The dropship's melody is one of the loudest sounds in the game — range 60 with volume 1.3 per the Audible Sounds data.
