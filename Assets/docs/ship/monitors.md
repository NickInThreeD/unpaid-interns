# Monitors

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Monitors

## Overview

The monitors are a fixed installation inside the autopilot ship and the core hardware for **camera duty** — the role of a crew member who stays aboard to track teammates and warn them of danger. They comprise three groups: radar cams, CCTV monitors, and quota monitors. The **brake lever** used to land and take off sits underneath them. The monitors cannot be moved.

## Key Points

- **Radar cams (left)** — top-down map around a selected employee.
- **CCTV monitors (right)** — live camera feed inside the ship and at its entrance.
- **Quota monitors (top)** — profit quota progress and deadline.
- The **brake lever** for landing and departure is beneath the assembly.

## Radar Cams

The main left-hand monitor shows a top-down map of the area surrounding whichever employee is selected. Two buttons sit to its left:

- **Upper (white)** — switches the selected player.
- **Lower (red)** — turns the monitor on or off. **This is the recovery control after a lightning strike disables the ship's electronics.**

The display can be duplicated at the terminal with `VIEW MONITOR`, and the switch button's function is duplicated by `SWITCH` or `SWITCH [crew mate]` — which is what lets one person run camera duty entirely from the terminal instead of running back and forth.

**While in orbit**, this monitor instead displays general information about the currently selected moon, including its current weather conditions.

## CCTV Monitors

The right-hand monitors show what the ship's interior and entrance cameras see. This is essential when the pressure door is closed and someone needs to check what is outside before opening it.

**Critically, the CCTV is the only way to detect the Ghost Girl** — she does not appear on the radar cams. She is visible on CCTV **even to crew members who are not the one being haunted**, making the monitors the crew's only shared warning of her presence.

## Quota Monitors

The pair of green-text monitors above the main screen show quota status:

- **Left screen** — the crew's current progress toward the profit quota.
- **Right screen** — the deadline, i.e. days remaining before scrap must be sold to The Company.

### Challenge Moon display

As of Update 47 (the Challenge Moons update), starting a session on a Challenge Moon changes both monitors to a bright purple backdrop. The left displays the challenge moon's name, and the right — having no day counter — reads **"AS MUCH PROFIT AS POSSIBLE"**.
