# Scanner (Echo Scanner)

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Scanner

## Overview

The Echo Scanner — usually just "the scanner" — is a feature built into every employee suit in *Lethal Company*, activated with **RMB**. It highlights scrap, entities, and points of interest with colored circles and is the primary tool for locating loot and orienting yourself. It requires no purchase, battery, or inventory slot.

## What It Highlights

- **Scrap — green circles.** Each item is identified along with its value in Company Credits. When multiple pieces are in view, their **combined total value** is displayed.
- **Entities — red circles.** Scanning a new entity uploads its creature file to the terminal's Bestiary.
- **Points of interest — blue circles.** These include the ship and the main entrance, and also identify objects aboard the ship such as the terminal, charging station, and storage.

## Notes and Practical Uses

- The scanner doubles as a **very weak light source** — enough for early-game hauling and for judging jumps over dark pits.
- **Scanning a corpse reads out how that employee was killed.** Note that "Cause Of Death: Gunshot" is ambiguous: it can mean either a Turret or a Nutcracker.

## Limitations and Warnings

- On some moons — **Rend and Dine** are named — the scanner reports the **wrong location for the main entrance** when you are close to it.
- The **Apparatus** is scannable but always shows its value as **"???"**. It always sells for 80 credits.
- **Gift Box contents cannot be scanned** until the box is opened.
- **Ghost Girls and Masked cannot be scanned at all**, and have no data recorded in the terminal.
- **Scanned value does not always equal sell price.** Keys, the Sticky Note, and the Clipboard are cited examples.
- The in-game Service Manual incorrectly states that scrap is highlighted yellow and points of interest green. These may have been the original colors before a change.
