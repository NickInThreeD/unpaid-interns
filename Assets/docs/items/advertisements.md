# Advertisements

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Advertisements

## Overview

Advertisements are a user-interface feature introduced in the Version 70 update of *Lethal Company*. They are pop-up promotions that intermittently appear on screen during play, drawing attention to discounted items or currently available Decor pieces in the Store. The page is a short reference (marked as a stub on the wiki) describing what the feature does and how employees tend to perceive it.

## Key Points

- Added in **Version 70**.
- At random moments during a day, an advertisement temporarily covers part of the **left side of every player's screen** — the ads are shown to all players, not just one.
- Ads promote either an **item currently on sale** or a **Decor** piece available in the Store.
- Intended as a convenience for players who don't regularly check the Store for rotating sales and decor stock.
- Experienced players generally treat them as a nuisance: they arrive unpredictably and add both visual and audio clutter at moments when attention matters.

## Important Details

There is no documented way to control when advertisements appear — timing is random within a day. Because they occupy screen space and play sound, they can interfere at inconvenient moments, which is the main practical limitation noted on the page. The feature is purely informational; it does not change prices, stock, or gameplay mechanics on its own, it only surfaces information that is otherwise available by checking the Store through the terminal.
