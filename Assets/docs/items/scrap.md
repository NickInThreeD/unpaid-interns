# Scrap

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Scrap

## Overview

Scrap is the main collectible in *Lethal Company* — the loot employees gather on abandoned moons and sell to The Company to fulfil profit quotas. This page documents how scrap spawns (rarity weights, special scrap, Single Item Days), the properties every scrap item shares, and the full list of regular and special scrap.

## Key Points

- Most scrap is found **inside the Facility**; some special scrap spawns on the moon's exterior.
- Employees carry **up to four pieces at a time**, matching their inventory slots.
- **Picking up a two-handed item locks the other slots** — you can carry the two-handed item plus up to three others only if the others were already in hand. Practically, the "Hands Full" state prevents picking anything else up.
- Prioritize **higher-value scrap**, but watch total weight: heavier loads slow you down significantly.

## The Loss Condition

**Unlike store items and special items, all scrap is lost when the whole crew dies or goes missing on a moon.** This includes ordinary scrap, dual-purpose items like the Stop Sign, and special scrap such as the Double-Barrel. Returning to the ship before conditions become dangerous is the entire risk calculation of the game.

## Scrap Spawning

### Regular scrap and rarity

Each moon has its own **scrap pool** listing every item that can spawn there alongside a **rarity** weight. A specific item's spawn chance equals **its rarity divided by the sum of all rarities in that moon's pool.**

This means **the same item can have completely different rarities on different moons**. The Large Axle, for example, is very common on Experimentation (high rarity relative to that pool) but rare on Rend (low rarity relative to that pool). Because raw rarity values are unintuitive, the wiki generally presents **spawn chances** instead.

### Special scrap

Special scrap is **not** governed by the moon's scrap table. Each has unique spawn criteria — a specific moon, a specific interior type, or a specific entity spawning first. Special scrap also does **not count toward** a moon's min/max scrap amount.

### Single Item Days

A **5.2% chance per day** that only one type of scrap spawns on the moon. The item is pulled from the moon's regular table **ignoring base spawn chances**. See the Single Item Day page.

## Scrap Properties

Every scrap item has:

- **Min / Max / Average Value** — the sell price at 100% value. Note that **items pulled from Gift Boxes can exceed the item's normal value range.**
- **Weight (lb)** — heavier items slow employees more. Stamina depletes faster and regenerates more slowly the greater the total carried weight.
- **Conductive** — whether lightning can strike it during stormy weather. Usually metallic objects.
- **Two-handed** — gives the "Hands Full" status, preventing holding anything else and locking out functions such as climbing ladders.
- **Most common on moon** — where you are most likely to find it. Does not apply to special scrap.

## Notable Regular Scrap

The highest-value regular items are the **Zed Dog** (0–199, avg. 99, weightless, most common on Rend — the widest value range in the game), **Cash Register** (80–159, avg. 119, but 84 lb and two-handed), **Rubber Ducky** (2–99, avg. 51, weightless), **Toilet Paper** (60–87, avg. 73 at only 5 lb), **Teeth** (60–83, avg. 71, weightless), and the **Robot Toy** (56–87, avg. 71).

The best **value-to-weight** picks are the weightless items: Zed Dog, Teeth, Rubber Ducky, Toy Cube, Toothpaste, Whoopie Cushion, Tongue. The worst are heavy low-value items like the **Yield Sign** (42 lb for avg. 27) and **Tattered Metal Sheet** (26 lb for avg. 15).

### Special properties

- **Apparatus** — removing it from its socket shuts off all Facility power and opens all secure doors, gives a **70% chance to set the minimum entity spawn to two** for subsequent cycles, and **activates every Old Bird on the moon**.
- **Bee Hive** and **Egg** — both have **distance-based value**: the further the spawn location from the ship landing area, the more they are worth.
- **Egg** — can **hatch into a baby Sapsucker**, which calls the Giant Sapsucker directly to wherever the egg is. Carrying one is actively dangerous.
- **Player Body** — costs **20% of total credits per unrecovered body, or 8% per recovered one**. Bodies add 5 credits to the moon's collected total and can be sold to The Company for 5.

## Trivia

The gameplay trailer refers to scrap as "worthless garbage", implying it is junk left behind by whoever previously inhabited these moons — yet The Company assigns each piece a specific valuation.
