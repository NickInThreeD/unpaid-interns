# Store (Company Store)

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Store

## Overview

The Company Store is the purchasing mechanic in *Lethal Company*, letting crews order equipment, ship upgrades, and furniture using Company Credits earned by selling scrap. It is accessed through the ship's terminal with the `STORE` command. This page covers ordering and delivery rules, the discount system, and the full equipment, upgrade, and decoration catalogues.

## Navigation and Ordering

Typing `STORE` prints the full item list, including the current quota period's rotation of ship decor and item sales.

- Buy by typing an item's name, or **at least its first three letters** — `FLA` for Flashlight, `PRO` for Pro-Flashlight — then confirm or deny (shortenable to `C` or `D`).
- Order **multiples of one item** by typing a number before or after the name. **Different items cannot be combined into one command.**

## Delivery Rules

Items ship to the current moon via the **Dropship**. Ordering from orbit holds the shipment until you land.

- Multiple purchases made quickly, or made before landing, are **batched into a single shipment**.
- **Once the delivery vehicle lands you have 30 seconds to retrieve everything.** The rocket leaves regardless, and **The Company does not refund incomplete deliveries.**
- Only **one delivery vehicle may land at a time**. Orders over **12 items**, or purchases with gaps between them, split across multiple vehicles, each queued to arrive shortly after the previous one departs.

## Discounts

Items randomly receive discounts. The severity and the number of discounted items vary daily, and almost every item can be discounted. When an item goes on sale, or new ship/employee decor enters the rotation, an **advertisement** may appear on an employee's visor.

**Notable behaviors from the terminal listings:**

- **Extension Ladder** reaches up to nine meters and **auto-stores itself after 18 seconds** to save batteries.
- **Stun Grenade** stuns most creatures for 5 seconds and detonates 3 seconds after the striker lever is pulled.
- **Zap Gun** delivers upwards of 80,000 volts. It only stuns while the current keeps flowing — you must pull the gun side to side to counter the beam's bend and stay on target.
- **TZP-Inhalant** greatly improves your ability to move long distances while carrying heavy objects, but the gas impacts the brain with extended exposure.
- **Radar-Booster** works with the `SWITCH` command (to view it on the main monitor, requires activation) and `PING` (to play a sound from it).
- **Belt Bag** holds 15 utility items; press [Q] to store, click to open and drag items out. **Other players can use the bag while it's fastened around your waist.**
- **Survival Kit** bundles 4 Flashlights, 4 Walkie-Talkies, and 2 Shovels for 138 credits.

**Six pieces of equipment are conductive** and therefore lightning hazards in stormy weather: Shovel, Extension Ladder, Radar-Booster, TZP-Inhalant, Zap Gun, and Jetpack.

## Ship Upgrades

Ship upgrades add permanent functions to the autopilot ship and **remain available until the crew is terminated**. Like decor, they can be moved into and out of storage.

- **Loud Horn — 100 credits.** Communicate with any crew member at any distance without a walkie-talkie; audible from anywhere.
- **Signal Translator — 255 credits.** Broadcast a text message under 10 letters to all crewmates via `TRANSMIT`.
- **Teleporter — 375 credits.** Teleports whoever is being monitored on the ship's radar; they lose all held items. About ten seconds to recharge.
- **Inverse Teleporter — 425 credits.** Teleports you to a random position outside the ship, dropping all items at the pad first. Usable by everyone at once, **3.5 minute cooldown**. Its disclaimer warns it **only transports in, not out — you may become trapped.**

## Ship and Employee Decorations

Purchasing a decoration permanently unlocks it for the rest of the run. **A crew cannot buy duplicates** — the terminal reports it as already unlocked. All decorations except **Cozy Lights** can be stored and retrieved with `STORAGE`.

**Player cosmetics (suits):** Decoy Suit (free, default orange), Brown Suit (free, anniversary), Green (60), Purple (70, free by default on Challenge Moons), Hazard (90), Bee (110), Bunny (200, makes a boing sound on jump but gives **no** jump boost), and Pajama (900).

**Furnishings:** Welcome Mat (40), Goldfish (50), Jack O' Lantern (50), Table (70), Dog House (80), Microwave (80, rotates single-handed items placed inside), Plushie Pajama Man (100), Record Player (120), Romantic Table (120), Television (130), Cozy Lights (140), Electric Chair (140, electrocutes employees), Disco Ball (150), Sofa Chair (150, sittable), Toilet (150), Shower (180), Fridge (225, stores single-handed items), and Classic Painting (400).

### Noise warning

**Jack O' Lantern and Disco Ball both alert Eyeless Dogs.** The Plushie Pajama Man squeaks but **does not**. Because a continuous noise source can hold an Eyeless Dog inside the ship indefinitely, the Disco Ball in particular is a real hazard.
