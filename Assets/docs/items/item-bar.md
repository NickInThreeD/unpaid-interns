# Item Bar

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Item_Bar

## Overview

The Item Bar — also called the Inventory — is the UI element in *Lethal Company* showing what an employee is currently carrying. It defines the hard limit on how much scrap a single trip can move, and the **two-handed item** rule it enforces is one of the most consequential constraints in the game. This page covers slot layout, controls, item effects, and the full list of actions blocked while carrying a two-handed object.

## Key Points

- **Four blue slots** at the bottom of the screen, each holding any item you can find.
- **One additional yellow slot** in the top-left, which can store most Store items and other zero-value objects — **with the notable exception of the Shovel**.
- Death drops all carried items on the ground.

## Item Loss on Death

Normally an employee drops everything they carry when killed. **The exceptions permanently delete the inventory**: being killed by an **Earth Leviathan**, **Forest Keeper**, or **Masked**, or **sinking in quicksand during Rainy weather**. In those four cases nothing is recoverable.

## Controls

- **[Scroll Up]** — move left along the inventory bar.
- **[Scroll Down]** — move right along the inventory bar.
- **[E]** — pick up or interact with objects.
- **[G]** — drop the currently held item.

## Object Effects

Some items carry interactive or passive effects:

- **Activatable** items like the Airhorn produce noise on use.
- **Passive noise** items like the Robot Toy emit sound simply from being picked up, and can only be silenced by repeatedly dropping and re-picking them up.
- **Light-emitting** items like the Fancy Lamp help in darkness.
- **Consumable** items like Keys disappear once used.

**Switching the item in your hand can stop an item's special effect** — the Robot Toy is the cited example.

## Two-Handed Objects

Picking up a two-handed item replaces the item bar with a **"HANDS FULL"** prompt. This does not merely occupy slots — it disables a specific list of interactions until the item is dropped.

### Still allowed while holding a two-handed item

- Entering and exiting the facility via the **main entrance or fire exit**.
- Opening and closing **regular unlocked doors**.
- **Ringing the Company bell** at the selling desk.
- Shutting off **steam valves**.
- Toggling **the ship's lights**.

### Blocked while holding a two-handed item

- Interacting with the **fusebox** (breaker box).
- Opening or closing the **item cupboard**.
- **Unlocking locked doors.**
- **Picking up any additional item**, even if slots are free.
- Changing suit at the **suit rack**.
- Opening or closing **the ship's doors**.
- Pulling **the ship's lever** to land or launch.
- Enabling, disabling, or switching **the ship's monitors**.
- Using **the ship's terminal**.
- Activating **either teleporter**.

The practical consequence is that a crew member hauling a two-handed item cannot operate the ship at all — they must set it down first, which matters when running a solo extraction or racing to launch before midnight.
