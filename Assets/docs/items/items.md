# Items

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Items

## Overview

This page is the top-level index of item categories in *Lethal Company*. Items divide into four groups — **Scrap Items**, **Store Items**, **Weapons**, and **Special Items** — each with different rules for acquisition, persistence, and how they affect entity behavior.

## Scrap Items

Loot objects found on moons. Collecting scrap is the employees' main objective and the only way to satisfy The Company.

**There are three ways to lose all scrap:**

1. The entire crew dies during a day.
2. The ship leaves at midnight with no crew members aboard.
3. Failure to meet the Profit Quota, which triggers disciplinary action — the crew is ejected into space. This is a game over.

## Store Items

Purchased at the terminal using company credits. Prices are fixed but **items sometimes go on discount**, which can make otherwise expensive equipment worth buying early.

## Weapons

A small subcategory drawn from both scrap and store items. Weapons matter mechanically beyond damage: **they are treated differently in Entity Targeting threat level calculations when an employee is holding one**. Most can damage entities and employees alike.

## Special Items

Anything that is neither scrap nor a store item. **Special items are treated like store items and are not lost when the entire crew dies.**

| Item | Value | Weight | Conductive | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clipboard | 0 | 0 lb | No | Instructions for new employees, found near the ship's monitor. A separate clipboard in the back of every Company Cruiser explains how to operate the vehicle. |
| Key | 3 | 0 lb | **Yes** | One-time use; unlocks a single locked door in the facility or a cottage. **Highly conductive — dangerous in stormy weather.** |
| Shotgun Shell | 0 | 0 lb | No | Two drop whenever a Nutcracker dies. Ammunition for the Double-Barrel. Uses the generic scrap icon. |
| Sticky Note | 0 | 0 lb | No | Sigurd's note, found on the wall near the terminal when starting or opening a save. Uses the generic scrap icon. |

All four are weightless and non-two-handed.
