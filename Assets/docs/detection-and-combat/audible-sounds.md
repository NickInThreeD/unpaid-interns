# Audible Sounds

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Audible_Sounds

## Overview

This reference page catalogues the sounds in *Lethal Company* that entities can perceive, and how loud and far-reaching each one is. Every noise in the game is described by two values: **noise range** (how far away it can be heard) and **noise volume** (how loud it plays). The page is intended for employees who want to understand which actions and items give away their position and which entities actually react to sound.

## Key Points

- **Range** = maximum distance at which a noise can be detected.
- **Volume** = the loudness value attached to the noise, used by entity hearing checks.
- Not all entities hear; those that do use sound in different ways — for targeting, for attention (turning to look), or for other behaviors entirely.

## Entities That Perceive Sound

- **Butler** — uses sound for **targeting**.
- **Eyeless Dog** — uses sound for **targeting**. This is its only means of finding prey.
- **Baboon Hawk** — **attention**; turns toward the noise.
- **Hoarding Bug** — **attention**.
- **Masked** — **attention**.
- **Spore Lizard** — **attention**.
- **Manticoil** — **alertness**; noise causes it to fly away.
- **Hygrodere** — reacts by **dancing**, but only to the Boombox.
- **The Company Monster** — sound affects its **patience and attack behavior**.

## Important Details

### Loudest noise sources

The most dangerous sounds by range are the **Dropship's locating melody** (range 60, volume 1.3 — the loudest event in the game), the **Airhorn** (range 65, volume ~0.95–1), and the **Clown Horn** (range 60). The **Loud Horn** at full volume reaches range 30, and the **Jetpack** in flight reaches 25. The **Company Monster's bell** has an unusually high volume of 1.25 at range 18.

### Employee-generated noise

Movement is a constant giveaway: sprinting has range 22 / volume 0.6, walking range 17 / volume 0.4, and landing after a fall range 7 / volume 0.5. Voice activity is the most variable of all, spanning range 3–36 and volume 0.6–0.9 depending on how loudly the player speaks. Dropping any held item produces range 8 / volume 0.5.

### Passive item noise

Some scrap makes noise merely by being carried, with reduced volume when unequipped:

- **Robot Toy** — range 15, volume 0.55 (the noisiest passive item).
- **Teeth** — range 10, volume 0.4.
- **Rubber Ducky** — range 10 on pickup or drop.
- **Old Phone** — range 4 on pickup.

### Tool and equipment noise

Notable entries include the Cash Register (range 25), Zap Gun (range 20, volume 0.92), Hairdryer (range 30), Jack O' Lantern (range 18), Radar Booster ping (range 12), Boombox while playing (range 16), Shovel impacts (range 17, volume 0.8), and Spray Paint (range 10). The Extension Ladder is unusually noisy across several separate events — opening the lid (range 18, volume 0.8), extending (range 8), hitting the ship's roof (range 17), and striking a wall (range 18).

Counterintuitively, the **Double-Barrel shotgun blast registers at only range 4 / volume 0.5** — far quieter to entities than an airhorn or even walking.

### Environmental and entity noise

Ambient sounds indoors and outdoors range from 0–4 with volume 0–0.5. Lightning strikes register range 4 / volume 0.5, as do in-game interface warnings and tips. Vow's fragile bridge creaks at range 4. Many entities are themselves noisy — Baboon Hawk footsteps and collisions (range 7–8), Thumper wall impacts (range 8, volume 0.75), Spore Lizard stomping (range 8, volume 0.75), and Manticoil flight (range 12).

## Warnings and Special Cases

- Activatable noisemakers such as the Airhorn use a **randomly chosen loudness and pitch each time**, so their effective range varies between uses.
- **Eyeless Dogs cannot hear ambient noise at all**, and ignore any noise with a volume below 0.25. This is why quiet movement and careful item handling can defeat them.
- **Baboon Hawks cannot detect their own noises or those of other Baboon Hawks**, so a troop does not distract itself.
