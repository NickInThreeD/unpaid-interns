# Fear

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Fear

## Overview

Fear — also described as the "shocked" condition — is an employee status effect in *Lethal Company* that has no official in-game name or documentation. It distorts the screen and plays an unsettling synth tone, but has **no direct mechanical penalty**. This page catalogues what triggers it, what does not, and why it is arguably more useful than harmful.

## Key Points

- When shocked, the employee's screen becomes **distorted and blurry for a few seconds**, affecting most of the UI including the Item Bar, HP bar, and item battery indicators.
- A jarring synth-like sound plays. **The sound has no world source and is heard only by the affected employee** — teammates hear nothing.
- Fear level drives only the overlay and audio. It does not reduce health, speed, stamina, or any other stat.

## Causes

### Critical health

Whenever an employee's HP drops below 20 they enter the critical health state and become feared simultaneously, regardless of how the damage was dealt. Both effects persist until health regenerates back to 20.

### Seeing a dead employee

Directly viewing another employee's Player Body causes fear. Notably, **teleporting a corpse back to the ship triggers fear for everyone inside the ship or otherwise in view of the body** — a common surprise for the person on camera duty.

Some deaths do *not* trigger it because the moment of death isn't witnessed: watching someone fall into water or quicksand and drown, deaths caused by The Company Monster, an employee transforming into a Masked, and being eaten by an Earth Leviathan.

### Entity interactions

Many entities have a dedicated fear trigger, generally tied to being targeted. If the entity responsible dies, the effect dissipates. Triggers include:

- A **Hoarding Bug** flying near an employee.
- A **Snare Flea** dropping to ensnare an employee. If it succeeds, fear persists until the employee is freed and far enough away; if it fails, fear persists until the flea stops pursuing.
- A **Thumper**, **Eyeless Dog**, or **Giant Sapsucker** targeting an employee.
- A **Coil-Head** freezing close enough to an employee.
- A **Bracken** being spotted mid-hunt — intensity scales with distance.
- A **Forest Keeper** beginning a chase. Merely being looked at by one produces a similar ambience without the full effect, which acts as an early warning.
- A **Masked** being interrupted mid-attack.
- A **Jester** popping — this affects all nearby employees at once.

### Entities with no fear interaction

Bunker Spider, Mask Hornets, Hygrodere, Ghost Girl, Spore Lizard, Nutcracker, Earth Leviathan, Baboon Hawk, Circuit Bees, Tulip Snake, Manticoils, and Roaming Locusts have no unique shock mechanic.

## Practical Effects

**Negative:** the effect can genuinely startle a player who doesn't understand it. Some players avoid entity encounters specifically to avoid being shocked, believing it has a larger gameplay impact than it actually does.

**Positive:** because the effect is triggered by being noticed or targeted, it functions as a free proximity alarm. The clearest example is the Forest Keeper, whose ambience plays whenever it looks in your direction — invaluable on moons with poor line of sight such as Rend, in foggy weather, or when sitting in the ship.

## Common Misconception

It is often assumed that certain entity attacks cause the shock condition directly. They usually do not — the attack simply pushes the employee into critical health, and *that* is what causes the fear. Bunker Spiders and Coil-Heads are cited as examples of this confusion.
