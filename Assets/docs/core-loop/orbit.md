# Orbit

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Orbit

## Overview

Orbit is the state of the autopilot ship whenever it is not landed on a moon's surface in *Lethal Company*. Every new season begins with the ship in orbit, and the ship returns to orbit between work days. Orbit functions as the game's planning and downtime phase: routing, purchasing, and inventory review all happen here.

## Key Points

- The ship is in orbit whenever it is **not on a moon's surface**.
- Every new season starts in orbit; the ship also returns to orbit at the end of each work day.
- **Radar cams are disabled** while in orbit.
- Crew members can **select a new moon** for the autopilot to route to.
- Items ordered from the Store while in orbit are **delivered at the beginning of the next work day**.

## Important Details

The terminal's `SCAN` command behaves differently in orbit. Instead of scanning the surroundings of a moon, it reports **the quantity and total value of every item currently inside the ship** — making it the standard way to check how much collected scrap you are carrying before deciding whether to sell.

Because radar cams are offline and no moon is loaded, orbit is a safe state with no entity threat; it is the natural point to review the quota, plan purchases, and pick the next destination.
