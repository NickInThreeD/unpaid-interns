# Time

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Time

## Overview

Time is the core scheduling mechanic of *Lethal Company*. Each day on a moon runs on a fixed, compressed clock that governs when entities spawn, when the ship departs, and how much of a facility a crew can realistically clear. Reading the clock — and knowing what each hour means — is fundamental to survival. This page covers how time advances, how it's displayed, its relationship to entity spawning, and practical time-management strategy.

## Key Points

- Time begins the moment the ship lands and **starts at 7:40 AM**; the ship is considered to arrive at **8 AM** and **always leaves at midnight**, whether or not every employee is aboard.
- A full day lasts **700 real seconds (11 minutes 40 seconds)**. One real second equals **1.4 in-game minutes**.
- Extra time cannot be added to a mission. The only way to change departure is to make it *earlier* — a living player starting the ship, or all spectators (dead players) voting to leave early.
- **The Company Building (71-Gordion) is the only destination where time does not progress**, so a crew can stay as long as it likes when selling.

## Time Display

The clock uses a 12-hour format with a.m./p.m. and can **only be viewed while outdoors** — not inside a facility. A symbol to the left of the time marks the phase of day:

- **Full sun** — 8:00 a.m. to noon.
- **Setting sun** — noon to 5:30 p.m.
- **Crescent moon** — 5:30 p.m. to 10:00 p.m.
- **Human skull** — 10:00 p.m. to midnight.

Phase transitions can be offset by roughly ±5 minutes due to time precision, so a boundary may read 5:25 p.m. or 5:35 p.m.

## Time Alerts

At **10:12 p.m.**, all employees not aboard the ship receive a warning that the ship departs at midnight. The same style of warning appears when spectators vote to leave early, giving employees one hour's notice.

## Entity Spawning and Time

Entity spawn probability is a function of the current time. Daytime entities are common early but their spawning drops sharply or stops after **3:00 p.m.**, at which point outdoor entities — generally far more hostile — take over.

Expected onset of outdoor entity spawning varies by moon:

- **11 a.m. – 1 p.m.:** Adamance, Artifice, Embrion
- **1 p.m. – 3 p.m.:** Rend, Dine, Titan
- **3 p.m. – 5 p.m.:** Vow, March
- **5 p.m. and later:** Assurance, Offense, Experimentation

## Strategy

- **The walkie-talkie as a clock.** A fully charged walkie-talkie turned on at the start of the day will be nearly flat by the end, so its battery level tells you the outdoor time while you're inside a facility: three-quarters charged ≈ 10 a.m.–noon; half charged ≈ 3–5 p.m.; one-quarter charged ≈ 10 p.m.–midnight.
- **Extraction timing.** On easier moons or early quota days, aim to have scrap back at the ship by around **3 p.m.**, the latest point before outdoor entities become likely. On harder moons or late quota days, pull that forward to about **1 p.m.**
- Factor weather in: stormy weather slows transfers, and flooded weather can seal off entrances and exits entirely.
- **Dedicate a runner.** Having one employee shuttle scrap to the ship entrance frees the rest to keep scavenging, widening the effective indoor window.
- **Avoid voting to leave early** where possible. It removes options from crewmates who may still have been able to return or survive.

## Trivia

- The **Barber** is the only entity that gets faster as the day progresses.
- There are four time-based sound cues — at the start of day, midday, sunset, and night — each more sinister than the last.
- If you are outside the ship early enough, you can watch the clock tick from 8:02 to 8:03 a.m. on the in-game time scale.
