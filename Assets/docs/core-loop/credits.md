# Credits

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Credits

## Overview

Company Credits are the in-game currency of *Lethal Company*, earned by selling scrap at the Company Building and spent on equipment, ship upgrades, and cosmetics from the Store. A credit balance persists for an entire game — it is only lost when the crew fails a profit quota and is discharged. This page covers how credits are earned, the time-based sell-value penalty, and the death fee.

## Key Points

- Symbol: the credit mark used throughout the wiki's value tables.
- A new contract starts the crew with **60 credits**.
- Credits carry across days and quota cycles for the whole run.
- Credits are spent at the **Store** (via the ship terminal) on equipment, ship upgrades, and decor.

## Earning Credits

The primary source is gathering scrap from moons and selling it at the Company Building on Gordion. **The Company actively penalizes selling early in the quota cycle** by paying a reduced percentage of scrap value:

| Days left | Day (as labelled on The Company page) | Buy rate |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Day 1 | **30%** |
| 2 | Day 2 | **53%** |
| 1 | Day 3 | **77%** |
| 0 | Day 4 (deadline) | **100%** |

*The Credits and The Company wiki pages describe the same schedule with opposite day labelling; the mapping above reconciles them.*

This creates the central tension of the sell timing: waiting until the deadline day maximizes the value of each piece of scrap, but the overtime bonus (see Profit Quota) rewards fulfilling the quota early. Exceeding the profit quota grants an overtime bonus of additional credits on top of the sale.

## The Death Fee

Every employee death costs the crew credits:

- **8% of the crew's total credits per death.**
- **An additional 12% per body not recovered**, for a total of **20%**.

Because the fee is percentage-based, losses scale with wealth — leaving multiple bodies behind when the crew is well-funded can be catastrophic.

**Recovery advice:** teleport dead crewmates back to the ship, or carry them manually if no teleporter is available. When teleporting a body, grabbing and dropping it is recommended so that it registers as being inside the ship. If a body becomes irrecoverable through game mechanics, the crew still pays the full 20% fine.
