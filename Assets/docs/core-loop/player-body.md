# Player Body

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Player_Body

## Overview

The Player Body — commonly called a **ragdoll** — is the special scrap item that a dead employee becomes in *Lethal Company*. It carries the deceased employee's cause of death (revealed by scanning) and, critically, determines the size of the insurance penalty deducted from the crew's credit balance. This page covers the body's properties, the recovery penalty, the causes of death, and the cases where a body is destroyed outright.

## Key Points

- **Type:** Special scrap. **Weight:** 11 lb. **Two-handed:** yes. **Conductive:** no.
- **Sell value:** 5 credits (only sellable in one specific case, below).
- When an employee dies, they become a ragdoll and **all inventory items drop at the death location**, with exceptions.
- A Player Body can be **scanned** to reveal the cause of death.

## The Insurance Penalty

Losing a crew member costs credits regardless of what you do — but recovering the body halves the loss:

- **Body returned to the ship:** 8% of the crew's total credits.
- **Body not returned:** an additional 12%, for a **total of 20%**.

This is the primary mechanical reason to retrieve corpses.

## Important Details

### Persistence and selling

Unlike normal scrap, a Player Body **does not persist when the ship leaves into orbit** — the dead employee respawns on entering space and the body disappears. The one exception: if an employee dies at **The Company** (Gordion) for any reason other than The Company Monster, their Player Body can be sold for credits like ordinary scrap.

### Bodies that cannot be recovered

If an employee is killed by a **Forest Keeper**, **Earth Leviathan**, **Masked**, or by sinking into quicksand during rainy weather, no Player Body spawns at all and **every item they were holding is permanently deleted**. Death to **The Company Monster** also leaves the body unretrievable, but in that case items are dropped rather than lost.

Death to a **Giant Sapsucker** segments the body so that none of it can be retrieved, making the full **20% fee unavoidable**.

### Visual effects on the body

Several killers leave distinctive marks:

- **Barber** — the body is cut in half; only the top half is collectable.
- **Bunker Spider** — the body (or one it picks up) is wrapped in web and hung from the ceiling, retrievable only with the teleporter.
- **Cadaver Bloom, Ghost Girl, Kidnapper Fox, or the Factory entrance fan** — the body is decapitated and the head comes off.
- **Coil-Head** — the body loses its forearms and its head is replaced by a spring.
- **Feiopar** — scratch marks appear across the body; these show up whenever a Feiopar attacked, regardless of the killing blow.
- **Hygrodere** — the gloves and boots vanish, leaving only the suit and no human material. The wiki notes this may hint that The Company values only the suit, not the person.
- **Old Bird flamethrower or Company Cruiser explosion** — the body appears charred.

## Causes of Death

Scanning a Player Body reports one of: **Abandoned** (left behind alive; shows as "MISSING" rather than "DECEASED"), **Blast**, **Bludgeoning**, **Burning**, **Crushing**, **Drowning**, **Electrocution**, **Fan**, **Gravity**, **Gunshots**, **Inertia**, **Kicking**, **Mauling** (a nonspecific entity kill), **Scratching**, **Snipped**, **Stabbing**, **Strangulation**, **Suffocation**, and **Unknown**.
