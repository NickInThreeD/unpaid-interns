# Guide: Contract

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Guide:Contract

## Overview

A contract — commonly called a "run" — is the entire span of a crew's employment with The Company in *Lethal Company*. It begins with onboarding and lasts a full season. Because performance expectations rise continuously, **every contract ultimately ends with the crew being fired (out of an airlock)**. This guide is written as a starting point for new hires and summarizes duties, provisions, safety, and the economics of the quota.

## Basic Duty and Provisions

The crew's job is to collect scrap from abandoned moon colonies and deliver it to The Company. They are issued:

- An **autopilot ship**
- **Suits equipped with Echo Scanners**
- Access to the **Bestiary** and the **Company Store**

The ship carries routing to **nine listed exomoons and two hidden ones** (`MOONS` at the terminal):

41-Experimentation, 220-Assurance, 56-Vow, 21-Offense, 61-March, 20-Adamance, 85-Rend, 7-Dine, 8-Titan, plus the hidden **5-Embrion** and **68-Artifice**.

The Company also offers shorter single-day contracts for teams willing to tackle a **challenge moon**.

**The autopilot always selects landing areas just outside either industrial or residential buildings** — you never land far from an objective.

## Team Safety

**Adverse weather** adds hazards to the landing area and can wreck a run before the crew even reaches the target building. Most weather is manageable with basic safety practice.

**Local wildlife** is the other major hazard. The standard-issue **Echo Scanner** ([RMB]) scans wildlife into the Bestiary, which is readable from the ship (`BESTIARY`) and provides survival advice for each creature.

**Expert crews assign one member to camera duty** to support the team from inside the ship using the terminal and monitors.

## Economy and Equipment

Profitable crews earn **Credits**, spendable at the Company Store (`STORE`) on equipment such as shovels and flashlights, ship upgrades, and decor. **A new contract starts with 60 credits.** Store deliveries arrive by rocket dropship at the landing area.

## Fines and Merits

- **Up to 20% of the crew's total Credits is charged per body not recovered.** Leaving multiple bodies behind can be enormously costly for a well-funded crew. **Deceased crewmates must be carried home or teleported to avoid the full fine.**
- **Lost or deceased crewmates are replaced before the next work day** — "You won't be able to tell the difference."
- Teams that produce high profits with minimal losses are graded more favorably, and **employee ranks are adjusted at the end of every work day**.

## Profit Quota Economics

The profit quota is the minimum scrap value The Company expects **every 4 days**. Importantly, **the crew keeps the Credits that go toward quota** and may spend them at the store — the quota is a threshold, not a tax.

Exceeding the quota earns an **overtime bonus**, starting at **15 credits over quota** and paying **1 credit per 5 credits over-delivered**. An additional **efficiency bonus** rewards delivering ahead of the deadline.

However, early delivery is deliberately discouraged, because The Company pays only partial value for scrap turned in early:

| Days left | Efficiency bonus | Buy rate |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | 45 credits | **30%** |
| 2 | 30 credits | **53%** |
| 1 | 15 credits | **77%** |
| 0 | 0 credits | **100%** |

The bonus never compensates for the buy-rate penalty, which is why crews normally hold their scrap until the deadline day.

## Termination

A crew that has not met quota before the deadline is subject to The Company's disciplinary process.
