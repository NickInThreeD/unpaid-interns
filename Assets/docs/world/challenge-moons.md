# Challenge Moons

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Challenge_Moons

## Overview

Challenge Moons — also called the Weekly Challenge — are *Lethal Company*'s competitive game mode. A randomized challenge moon is generated each week with modified gameplay variables and a fixed seed shared by every player. Crews get **one day** to collect as much scrap as possible, with no profit quota, and their score is submitted to a global leaderboard. Challenge moons use a separate save file from the normal campaign.

## Key Points

- Uses a **separate save file**, shown as a fourth save slot in the main menu.
- Rotates to a new challenge **at midnight UTC at the start of Monday**.
- **The same seed is used for everyone**, making runs directly comparable.
- Each challenge moon borrows its exterior from one of the core moons but has its own designated name.
- **There is no quota.** The ship's quota monitors simply read **"AS MUCH PROFIT AS POSSIBLE."**
- Employees do **not** need to sell at The Company — scrap totals are displayed automatically once you depart the moon successfully.
- Modifiable variables include scrap amount, entity spawns and power levels, hazard amount, weather, and interior type.

## Modifiers

### Starting credits

Instead of the usual 60 credits, there is a 50/50 split between two ranges:

- Anywhere between **20 and 300** credits.
- Anywhere between **20 and 900** credits.

### Spawn chance modifier

Each of the four spawnable categories has a **45% chance** to have one member's spawn chance altered:

- **Scrap:** one item from the moon's pool has its rarity raised to 100.
- **Indoor entities:** one indoor entity has its rarity raised to 100.
- **Outdoor entities:** one outdoor entity has its rarity raised to 100.
- **Hazards:** one hazard (Landmine, Turret, or Spike Trap) has its spawn rate doubled.
- **Objects:** one exterior object type (large rocks, trees, pumpkins) gets an additional 12 spawned.

### Spawn rate modifiers

- Scrap spawn amount is increased by a random number between **10 and 30**.
- Indoor and outdoor entity power levels are each increased by a random number between **0 and 8**, allowing more entities overall.
- Indoor entity spawn rate is raised to produce, on average, **one additional entity per spawn cycle**.

### Weather modifier

Each of the two weather variables has a **20% chance** to be multiplied by a value between **0.4 and 1.6**. The effect depends on the weather: for Flooded it changes how fast water rises, for Stormy it changes how often lightning strikes, and for Eclipsed it changes the minimum number of entities spawned at the start of the day.

## Notes and Limitations

- **The leaderboard saves your most recent run, not your best run.**
- *Lethal Company* has no anti-cheat, so the top of the leaderboard is dominated by modded runs and is not representative of genuine player performance.
- Changing your device's date and time lets you access past or upcoming challenge moons.
- Employees default to the **purple suit** for the duration of a challenge moon.

## Trivia

- The first three challenge moons were playable only in the Version 47 public beta.
- **Voiumya-10** was the first challenge moon available at release. A bug allowed the unused **Lasso Man** entity to spawn there; it was patched immediately.
