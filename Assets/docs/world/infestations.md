# Infestations

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Infestations

## Overview

Infestations are a rare daily event added in **Version 65** that reshapes an interior's entity population around a single species. When one triggers, indoor power level and minimum indoor spawns are raised while one entity's spawn rate is massively increased and every other entity's is suppressed — producing a facility overrun by one creature. Only two entities can currently infest: **Hoarding Bugs** and **Nutcrackers**.

## Key Points

- Affects a **single day** only.
- Three simultaneous changes: **indoor power level increased**, **minimum indoor entity spawn increased**, and **one entity's spawn rate greatly increased while all others are greatly reduced**.
- **Infestations have no effect on outdoor entity spawn rates** — only the interior is affected.

## Trigger Chance

At the beginning of any given day the game rolls a **2.9% chance** for an infestation. There is one documented exception: **on October 23rd the chance drops to 1.5%**.

## Choosing the Infestor

Once an infestation is triggered:

- **Hoarding Bug: 75% chance.**
- **Nutcracker: 25% chance.**

The game then checks whether the selected entity can actually spawn on the current moon. **If one of the two cannot spawn there naturally, the other is guaranteed.** For example, Rend cannot spawn Hoarding Bugs, so Rend can only ever have Nutcracker infestations.

## Power Levels

The two infestation types are not equally severe:

- **Hoarding Bug infestation: power level 30.**
- **Nutcracker infestation: power level 20.**

For reference, a normal moon's maximum indoor power ranges from about 4 to 18, so an infestation raises the interior's entity budget far above anything ordinarily possible — which is why a facility can fill with dozens of the infesting creature.
