# Out of Bounds Areas

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Out_of_bounds_areas

## Overview

Out of bounds areas are regions of a moon's terrain that exist outside the normally playable space. They are generally unreachable on foot because hills, fences, or other terrain block the way, and are typically accessed with a **Jetpack**. This is a short, informal wiki page primarily documenting what these areas look like rather than any gameplay function.

## Key Points

- Out of bounds areas are **not normally accessible** — the surrounding terrain (hills, fences) prevents travel there on foot.
- A **Jetpack** is the standard means of reaching them.
- They contain no objectives, scrap, or gameplay content; they are leftover terrain outside the intended play space.
- Eventually the terrain terminates at a hard cut-off into the void.

## Documented Areas

### Vow

Vow is highlighted as having the most interesting out-of-bounds terrain, ranging from circular hill formations to very high green hills. The wiki's gallery documents a progression outward: a first terrain layer, a flat desert region, large hills, the tops of those hills, and finally the cut-off "void" that marks the edge of the map.

## Notes

The page is brief and informally written, and only Vow is described in any detail. Because these areas serve no gameplay purpose, reaching them costs mission time and risks stranding an employee far from the ship — the Jetpack fuel and travel time are not recoverable if something goes wrong.
