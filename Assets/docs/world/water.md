# Water

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Water

## Overview

Water is an outdoor terrain element in *Lethal Company*, split into two distinct kinds: **terrain water**, which is a permanent part of certain maps, and **weather water**, which appears only during Flooded weather as a rising or falling flood. This page documents where each appears, how water affects employees, entities, items, and the Company Cruiser, and how to escape it.

## Key Points

- **Terrain water** exists on three moons: Vow, March, and Adamance.
- **Flooded water** can occur on all moons **except Rend, Titan, and Embrion**.
- Despite the name, **rainy weather produces no water at all** — it produces quicksand, which behaves differently.
- Employees **cannot swim**: there is no vertical movement underwater.

## Terrain Water

**56-Vow** has a narrow river half-surrounding the map. The narrowest sections can be sprint-jumped, but most of it has steep edges that make climbing out difficult.

**20-Adamance** has a long, wide river widening into a deep lake with five exposed stones — four in a square and a fifth on the extension of one axis, which happens to sit on the line between the main entrance and the ship. The banks are too high to climb out of, but the water is decorative and sits at the map edge, so the normal route never crosses it.

**61-March** is the only map where terrain water lies **between the ship and the main entrance**, and the only one where terrain water is split into separate regions. A large lake sits in a deep pit directly on the straight line from ship to entrance — falling in makes climbing out impossible, though other escape routes exist. Two more lakes sit at 45-degree angles ahead of the ship's bow, near two different fire exits, and are likewise impossible to walk out of.

## Flooded Water

Flooding starts low and worsens through the day, typically **peaking around 5:00 p.m.**, though building entrances can be inundated earlier. On **Adamance and Dine** the flood is *reversed* — starting high and draining over time (as of the version documented; note the Weather page records that v80 removed this behavior from Adamance and applied it to Dine).

## Effects

### On employees

- No swimming — no vertical ascent or movement underwater.
- Horizontal movement is slowed, and gravitational acceleration underwater is lower than in free fall.
- **Stamina drains slowly even when wading through shallow water.**
- With the camera underwater, vision gains a filter and all sound is distorted and muffled.
- Falling into water that is too shallow, or falling from a great height, can still cause fatal fall damage.
- **Drowning occurs after 10 seconds underwater.** At 7 seconds the life support system issues an **"Oxygen Critical"** warning. Getting the head above water **instantly** restores oxygen.

### On entities

- **Every entity except the Masked can survive underwater.** The Masked drowns after ten seconds like an employee, and its AI actively avoids water — it only ends up submerged by failing to brake or by rising flood levels.
- **Movement of all entities is unaffected by water**, including flying ones. A common example is Tulip Snakes on flooded Adamance flying freely underwater to attach to employees.
- **Vision and hearing are not reduced by water** for any entity — unlike fog. Eyeless Dogs, Baboon Hawks, Masked, and Manticoils all detect sound underwater normally.
- Bee Hives spawn underwater and Circuit Bees survive there. Old Bird artillery fires and travels normally underwater despite flames logically being extinguished. Roaming Locusts and Manticoils tend to avoid water but can still fly through it.

### On items and scrap

- **All items and scrap sink** rather than float.
- All items function normally, but light-emitting items are dimmed — Flashlight, Pro-Flashlight, Radar-Booster, and glowing scrap such as the Apparatus, Fancy Lamp, and Laser Pointer.
- **Conductive items still attract lightning during stormy weather even while submerged.**

### On the Company Cruiser

Most Cruiser functions work underwater, but **steering is heavily distorted**. Remaining submerged slowly lowers the Cruiser's health, which can be repaired with Weed Killer.

### On interactive elements

Flooded entrances remain usable, and cottages flood along with the outdoors but can still be interacted with normally.

## Strategy

- Rend, Titan, and Embrion never have water at all.
- **A Jetpack is the single most effective answer to flooded moons** — it avoids drowning entirely.
- Experimentation, Assurance, and Offense have no terrain water, and their rugged terrain and exposed rocks provide plentiful high ground even at maximum flood.
- Artifice is easy to handle because its three open warehouses have second floors.
- **Without a Jetpack, do not land on Adamance or Dine during flooded weather** — water is highest at the start of the day and there is barely a safe route from the ship to the main entrance.
- On Vow and March, watch every downhill path: the slope may be too steep to climb back out. On Vow and Adamance, be careful crossing the fragile bridges.

### Escaping water

If you **walked** in rather than fell, back out immediately. If that fails, hug the shore, let stamina recover, then sprint out. The one noted exception is falling into Vow's canyon during a flood — there, rushing to the (submerged) fire exit and entering it is viable.

If you cannot walk out:

- **Extension Ladder** — the most cost-effective and reliable option at 60 credits, usable by you or dropped by a teammate.
- **Jetpack** — a teammate drops one in for you to fly out. Realistic only in the late game.
- **Teleporter** — someone on the ship can pull you out in seconds.

If death underwater is certain, drop your scrap at the shore so teammates can retrieve it. On Adamance, throw scrap far from the Baboon Hawk nest, since hawks steal scrap and relocate it — **unless** your teammates lack an Extension Ladder or Jetpack, in which case throwing it *toward* the hawks is better, because they may carry it out of the water for you.
