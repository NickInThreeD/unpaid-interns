# Fire Exit

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Exit

## Overview

A fire exit is the alternative way in and out of a facility or manor in *Lethal Company*, complementing the main entrance. Its **interior** placement is random, while its **exterior** location is fixed and consistent per moon. Most facilities have exactly one. Using a fire exit often provides a shorter or safer route than the main entrance, which is why it features heavily in moon-specific strategies.

## Key Points

- **Exterior locations are consistent** for each moon; **interior locations are random.**
- Most facilities have **one** fire exit — **61-March is the exception with three.**
- 71-Gordion has **none**, as it has no interior at all.

## Interior Occurrence

Inside, a fire exit can appear in:

- **Staircases**
- **Gap rooms**
- **Storage rooms**
- **Any room in the manor** — where **the exit can be hidden by libraries/bookshelves**

**Warning:** fire exits are **not guaranteed to have an unlocked line of doors** connecting them to the rest of the interior. Entering by fire exit can strand you behind locked doors, which is why bringing a Key or Lockpicker is standard advice for fire-exit entries.

## Notes

**Employees face the door as soon as they enter through a fire exit**, meaning they are momentarily turned away from the room. This leaves them vulnerable to threats that punish not looking — **Coil-Heads** in particular.
