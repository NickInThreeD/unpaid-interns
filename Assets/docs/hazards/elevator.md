# Elevator

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Elevator

## Overview

The Elevator is a mechanic in *Lethal Company* that was originally designed for level transitions, cut before release, then reworked and reintroduced with the **Mineshaft interior in Version 60**. In its current form it is a small elevator at the Mineshaft's main entrance that carries employees down to the rest of the interior.

## Game Mechanics

- Appears at the **main entrance of every Mineshaft interior**.
- Has **three buttons**: one inside the elevator and one on each floor beside it. The Mineshaft entrance room also includes a recall button so an employee left behind can call it back.
- **Items can be transported** on it.
- **You can stand on top of the elevator on the way down**, but riding the top on the way up **traps you in the area above**.
- **Masked entities can use the elevator exactly as a player can. No other entity can.**

## Original Design Concept

The elevator was originally intended as the transition into every level, rather than walking straight in. It was cut for three reasons: **simplicity, preventing player separation, and the inconvenient protection it offered against enemies.**

Remnants survive in the game: looking through the windows on the main facility doors on **Experimentation** reveals traces of the hallway that would have led to the original elevator. The original design can be seen in the game's first trailer.

The reintroduced Version 60 elevator is **noticeably smaller** than the original concept, exclusive to Mineshafts, and functions quite differently from the trailer version.

## Audio

The elevator has multiple jingle variations plus a running sound. The blue-lit cave entrances in the Mineshaft emit a **higher-pitched version of the elevator noise**, which employees use to locate cave entrances.

## Notes

- Parts of the elevator were reused to furnish the surrounding environment.
