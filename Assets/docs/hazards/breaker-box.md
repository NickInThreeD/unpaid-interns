# Breaker Box

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Breaker_Box

## Overview

The Breaker Box — also called the Power Switch or fusebox — is the device controlling a facility's electrical system in *Lethal Company*. It is a grey box with an openable lid containing **five red single-pole-double-throw switches**. Restoring power requires getting all five into the correct position, which is why an unpowered facility is a meaningful obstacle rather than a trivial fix.

## How It Works

- Open the lid at any time to change switch states.
- **Power is connected only when all five switch blades face left.** Any other configuration cuts power.
- With five two-position switches there are **32 possible combinations, and only one connects the facility's circuits.**

The box emits a distinctive **humming sound**, which helps locate it.

**Critically: if an employee removes the Apparatus, the Breaker Box stops working entirely** — power is cut for the facility as a whole and cannot be restored.

## Spawn Locations

**In the Factory**, breakers spawn in:

- Main Entrance Room
- Staircase
- Apparatus Room
- Server Room
- Storage Room

**In the Mansion**, breakers can spawn in most rooms.

(The Interior page adds that in the **Mineshaft**, dead ends are more likely to contain a Breaker Box than other room types.)

## Effects of Cutting Power

### Automatic / Secure Doors

**All automatic doors open due to loss of electricity**, and — importantly — employees on camera duty **can no longer open or close them through terminal commands**. Any entity contained behind a Secure Door is released.

### Lights

Most facility lights shut off. **The Yellow Office room is the exception — its lights are unaffected by both the Breaker Box and the Apparatus.** Employees without flashlights will struggle to navigate a blacked-out facility.

### Ghost Girl interaction

The **Ghost Girl temporarily overrides the breaker**: she turns already-off lights on and already-on lights off. While her influence is active, **the Breaker Box has no control over those lights** — it only resumes functioning once her effect ends.

## Warnings

- A Breaker Box in the Factory **can spawn with some switches already off**, leaving parts of the map unpowered from the start of the day until manually fixed.
- **Landmines and Turrets continue to function with no power**, despite being electronics — they still require the ship's terminal to disable temporarily.
- Removing the Apparatus makes the entire power system permanently unrecoverable for that run.
