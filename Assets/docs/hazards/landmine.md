# Landmine

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Landmine

## Overview

Landmines are stationary explosive map hazards in *Lethal Company* that detonate when an employee steps on and then **off** them, teleports off them, or strikes them with a weapon. The blast kills or injures employees and entities alike. The delayed trigger — reacting to release rather than pressure — is what makes them survivable and, with coordination, exploitable.

## Key Points

- **Damage:** 50–100, area of effect.
- **Instant death within 5.7 units** of the detonation; **50 damage between 5.7 and 6.4 units**.
- Triggered by: stepping off, teleporting off, hitting with a Shovel or similar, and — **inconsistently** — dropping scrap or another item on it.
- **Chain reactions:** any landmine within **6 units** of a detonating mine can also be set off, which in clusters can cascade dramatically.
- Can be temporarily disarmed from the ship's terminal via its special code.

## Warning Signs

A landmine **beeps every couple of seconds and flashes red**. When an employee steps on one, it makes a distinct **click** noise. Landmines can easily be mistaken for a **Garbage Lid** by an inattentive employee.

## The Standing-Still Rescue

Because the mine only triggers on release, **an employee who steps on one and does not move is safe indefinitely.** From that position, a crewmate on ship duty can either:

- **Deactivate it at the terminal**, or
- **Teleport the employee back to the ship** — though the resulting explosion can kill both employees and entities within the blast range.

**Critical caveat:** you must **be standing on the landmine** when asking someone to deactivate it. Walking through an already-"deactivated" mine will still explode it, because the deactivation window is very short.

## Safe Detonation

Items usable to trigger a mine from a distance include **Extension Ladders**, spent **Stun Grenades**, **Soccer Balls**, and **Easter Eggs**.

**Artifice is the most heavily mined moon** by average, with Dine matching its maximum of 35. **Rend is nearly mine-free.**

## Notes

- **Entities cannot detonate landmines on their own**, but they die if caught in the blast radius. However, **entities carrying a player body — such as Hoarding Bugs and Brackens — can set off mines**, because player bodies do trigger them.
- The model is visually based on the 90mm M12 series of inert practice anti-tank landmines.
