# Curtained Door

**Source:** https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Curtained_Door

## Overview

Curtained Doors are lever-operated sliding doors that serve as the main entrances to the four outdoor warehouses on **68-Artifice**. They open and close horizontally, and when closed they form a hard barrier against both entities and projectiles — making them the single most important defensive tool available on the game's hardest moon, primarily for containing Old Birds.

*(Note: Experimentation's rolling shutter door in front of the main entrance is a different mechanic, driven by a hidden pass-through detection probability.)*

## Key Points

- Found only on **Artifice**, one per outdoor warehouse.
- Open and close **horizontally**.
- **A closed Curtained Door prevents any entity from passing through.**
- **Projectiles cannot pass through either** — including Old Bird missiles.

## The Lever

Each Curtained Door has a corresponding lever. The lever has **no usage limit** and can be operated freely to keep the door open or closed. Pulling it displays an orange progress circle, the same style used when entering or exiting the main entrance.

**Initial states vary:** some doors spawn open and some closed. **The inaccessible warehouse on Artifice is always closed.**

## Uses

### Trapping Old Birds

The primary purpose. **On landing on Artifice during non-eclipsed weather, the strongly recommended first action is to enter every outdoor warehouse and inspect the interior.** If an inactive Old Bird is found inside:

1. Immediately close that warehouse's Curtained Door.
2. Leave through one of the other exits.

The Old Bird is then trapped and cannot attack once it activates — removing the moon's deadliest outdoor threat for the entire day.

If no Old Bird is present at the start of the day, **leave the door open** so the warehouse remains available as a trap for an entity later.

### Escaping pursuit

The most effective escape from a chasing entity — Old Bird, Eyeless Dog, Forest Keeper — is to enter an **unoccupied** warehouse, close the Curtained Door behind you, and leave via an alternative exit. Each warehouse has a different layout, which itself helps with evasion.

## Warning

**A closed Curtained Door blocks the Ghost Girl's line of sight, but if she is actively chasing you she will still pass straight through it.** Curtained Doors are not a defense against the Ghost Girl.
